Can the fret blend into the scallop? (true scale)

The circle is a fingertip pad (radius .30") rolling toward the fret. A stock 6230 meets the board vertically, so a finger can't reach the corner at its base. It bridges the corner and tips onto the crown, which is the click you feel. The wave fret's concave flanks are larger than the fingertip, so the finger rides the whole curve and its path never kinks. That's the one tested rule: every concave surface is larger than a fingertip, and only the crest the string touches is small. The rigid-finger model is a proxy, and pad radius is an assumption.

Taper: three rules compared

Radius-locked (recommended): depth grows in the same ratio as the fingerboard radius. Both come from one cone whose apex sits about 82" ahead of the nut, so every cross-section is the same family of shapes. It lands at .82" → .93" at the 12th, inside vintage range. The width-proportional rule gets too chunky (≈.99"), and a straight linear taper is the usual factory default.
