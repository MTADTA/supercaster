# final_carve.py v3.2: model prep before CNC (2026-11-12)

The outline, carve, edge, f-hole, pickguard and hardware positions are unchanged, so the top and back surfaces are the same as v3.1.

**One conflict blocks CNC.** The top slot as specified is wider than the bridge plate and has a treble offset. The PLATE plus GUARD do not cover it: a strip of 0.178 in² beside the plate would be visible, which breaks "no visible change". I kept the dimension as tasked and flagged it rather than narrowing it. See open item 1.

## 1. Changes

### 1.1 Trem routes (replaces `trem_cavity` x 20.4-26.1)
The route dimensions were measured from third-party 1:1 plan sheets of a commercial guitar and from a retailer's
installation sheet. Those numbers are not published here. The code reads them from the plan file (`TREM`), and this
section of the report has been cut down to what changed.
- Every route is a rounded rectangle with a 0.25 corner radius. The new function is `route_sdf(x, y, which)`.
- There are three routes: a top through-slot, a rear block cavity, and a spring cavity, placed from the pivot line.
- The slot and block cavity are offset toward the treble side, as on the plan sheets [computed].
- There is no rear cover plate (`rear_cover=None`). The back opening is the spring cavity plus the block cavity (`rear_mouth_sdf`).
- `trem_cover_lip` is replaced by `trem_mouth_pad` = 0.35, the flat back kept round the open mouth. The value is unchanged.

### 1.2 Pickguard screw bosses
- Screw positions come from `SCREWS` in the plan file (not published).
- A boss is added at every screw whose 0.25 boss plus 0.125 root fillet reaches the chamber footprint.
- **Four screws qualify, not three.**

| Screw | Distance into chamber at the screw | sdf_guard (inside the guard) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | +0.075 | 0.215 | over the chamber edge |
| B | +0.775 | 0.212 | on the top-skin stress peak in sim3d-REPORT §1.4 |
| C | +0.100 | 0.210 | over the chamber edge |
| D | -0.075 | 0.275 | 0.075 outside the chamber. A 0.5 boss there overlaps the chamber by 0.175, so it is included. |

All values above are [computed].

- **Geometry:**
  - Each boss is a solid column 0.50" in diameter hanging from the chamber roof to z = -0.65, with a 0.125 concave root fillet.
  - The bosses hang from the roof only. None runs to the back, because the final simulation had no top-to-back post.
- **Why the boss bottom is at -0.65:**
  - A #4 x 1/2" vintage guard screw through a 0.06-0.09" guard reaches about 0.44 into the wood [judged: recollection of the usual vintage spec; not looked up, so measure Jordan's screws].
  - That leaves about 0.2 of solid wood below the tip [judged].
  - The bosses stay in the top half: -0.65 is above the split at -0.90.
- **Effect on the chamber:**
  - The bosses take 0.23 in³ out of the chamber: 53.599 to 53.369 in³ [computed].
  - That raises the air resonance by about +0.6 Hz, using f ∝ V^-½ [computed], which is negligible against the ±25 Hz trim window.
  - Added wood is about 2-3 g in total [computed, density 0.42 g/cm³ judged].
- **Clearance to the liner:** 0.37 at screw B, 0.29 at screw A, and more at the other two [computed].

### 1.3 Liner seat
The numbers come from section 0 of `acoustic-hybrid.md` and `acoustics/final/liner-RESULT.md` [computed: liner-RESULT.md].
- **Liner geometry:**
  - The inner wall is the f-hole outline inset by 2.7 mm. The outer wall is 2.0 mm outside the outline.
  - The square ledge sits at the veneer underside, 5.6 mm (0.221") below the top.
  - The liner runs 0.75" below that, so its bottom is at z = -0.971.
- **The seat:**
  - Over the liner footprint plus a 0.06 glue land [judged], the roof is held at exactly `top_skin` 0.197.
  - That means no frame swell and no corner fillet there.
  - Nothing is recessed into the skin, because any recess would take the skin below `wood_min_3d` 0.190.
  - The 0.6 mm veneer lies flat on the seat and the liner glues to the veneer.
- **Liner solid:** the model now carries the liner's plan mask and its z range in `chamber_interior()` (`liner`, `liner_top`, `liner_bot`). The render draws it blackened.
- **Measured fit [computed]:**
  - The seat is flat: 0.197 to 0.197.
  - The footprint is inside the chamber, at least 0.502 from the chamber wall. Frame swell only reaches 0.25.
  - The liner bottom is 0.071 below the split. It hangs into the back half's open cavity, which is buildable but needs noting for the dry clamp.
- **Gap from liner bottom to chamber floor:** 0.277" (7.0 mm) [computed], against 7.4 mm in [computed: liner-RESULT.md]. Both are positive and the difference is the floor model.
- **Wood volumes [computed]:**
  - The liner wood is 2.00 in³, about 14 g, which matches liner-RESULT's ~14 g.
  - The veneer is 1.09 in³.
  - Throat area is 1.735 in² against 3.258 in² for the f-hole.

### 1.4 Set-neck tongue
- **What the model represents:** the joint only as a 2-D footprint. There is no 3-D tongue, so I added parameters and checks and documented the rest.
- **New parameters:** `tongue_end` = 21.7 and `tongue_w` = 1.90.
  - `tenon_end` (18.72) is unchanged and now only bounds the heel-fade box of the back carve. This is why the back carve did not move.
  - `HEEL_BLEND` is updated to match.
- **Clearances [computed]:**
  - 0.137 from the tongue end to the spring-cavity front wall.
  - 0.975 from the tongue footprint to the chamber.
- **Unresolved, z section:**
  - At mortise depth (-0.625), both single-coil routes cut through the tongue, since the render draws them about 0.75 deep [judged].
  - Below the routes, the tongue crosses the split at -0.90.
  - The 0.137 gap only matters if the tongue goes below the spring roof at -1.125. The sim's tongue did; the sim report notes the old cavity cut into it.
- **Document inconsistency (doc not edited):**
  - Section 0 of `acoustic-hybrid.md` still says the tongue ends "at the spring-cavity front wall (~x 20.3)", which came from the old wrong cavity. Its "Model work" line says ~21.7.
  - Section 3 still says the tenon stops 0.10 before the neck pickup route.

## 3. Open items
1. **The top slot as sourced would be visible.**
   - A strip about 0.30 wide on the treble side lies outside the bridge-plate box and behind the guard cutout.
   - The slot width I used rests on a single path in the plan sheets. That path spans exactly the block-cavity width, so it may be the block outline rather than the top slot [judged].
   - Before CNC, measure the real plate width and its offset, and the real top slot. Then either narrow `trem.slot` to sit under the plate, or correct PLATE in the plan data.
   - A hidden slot must still clear the trem block. Sizing it needs the block width and position, which section 0 already lists under "Waiting on Jordan". Measuring Jordan's block and plate settles this item.
   - Nothing was changed here, because the task fixed the slot width.
2. **The veneer meets the bosses.** The 0.6 mm cross-grain veneer under the roof is interrupted by all four bosses. Choose one:
   - notch the veneer round each boss, or
   - keep it continuous and glue each boss below it.

   No geometry was changed for this.
3. **The f-hole cleats meet the liner.** Section 0 calls for maple cleats under both f-hole ends (1.5 mm, 0.9 x 0.7 in). These overlap the liner footprint and seat at the tips. Either notch the cleats round the liner, or let the liner's outer wall stand in for them. The cleats have no acoustic effect per liner-RESULT.
4. **Tongue z section.** The depth and top are not modelled. See 1.4.
5. **Not done in this pass** (they are in section 0 but were not in this task): the 22-fret neck with round heel, and the glue seam on the forearm edge roll.
